FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. CopyIT is MIT licensed, with no paid tier, ads or account. It's built by PinnLabs in the open.

macOS says the app can't be opened.

The app is ad-hoc signed. Right-click it and choose Open the first time, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ClipboardManager.app. Installing with Homebrew avoids this.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Does it sync between devices?

No. Your history stays on this Mac on purpose.

Where is my data stored?

History metadata lives in UserDefaults and images in Application Support, both inside your user account.
